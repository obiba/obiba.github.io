---
title: "BBMRI-LPC website launched"
date: "2014-11-21"
summary: "Launch of the new BBMRI-LPC Exposure Data Library powered by Mica."
---

<p>The Biobanking and Biomolecular Resources Research Infrastructure – Large Prospective Cohorts (BBMRI-LPC), one of the
  largest biobanking networks in Europe, launched in collaboration with Maelstrom-Research its new data portal <a
    href="http://mineral.iarc.fr/mica/">http://mineral.iarc.fr/mica/ </a>
  powered by the OBiBa Mica software. This first version of the BBRMRI-LPC catalog comprises 14 studies, 28 annotated
  datasets and over 18,000 variables. </p>
