## Free online support

Free (but limited) support is provided in the GitHub repository of each application: go to [OBiBa on GitHub](https://github.com/obiba), select the application's repository and open an issue (GitHub account required).

## Paid support

Paid support is available to develop new features, improve existing ones, and assist with deploying OBiBa software. We are also open to new scientific collaborations and joint grant applications. Contact:

- **In Canada**: [Maelstrom Research](https://www.maelstrom-research.org/contact), [info@maelstrom-research.org](mailto:info@maelstrom-research.org)
- **In France**: [Epigeny](https://www.epigeny.io/), [info@epigeny.io](mailto:info@epigeny.io)
