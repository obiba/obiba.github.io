OBiBa is a suite of stand-alone applications that support the data management activities of epidemiological studies. These modular applications can be integrated into a comprehensive information management and analysis system for individual studies.

As part of the [Maelstrom Research](https://www.maelstrom-research.org) program, OBiBa includes advanced components for data harmonization and federation, for study networks that harmonize and securely share data among their members.

OBiBa applications are highly customizable and easy to use, built on current technologies, and comply with the highest standards of privacy and data security.

## History

OBiBa was launched in 2007 as a core project of the [Public Population Project in Genomics and Society (P3G)](https://p3g2.org/). The project grew rapidly and obtained substantial financial support from its main partners to build a new generation of open source software for data collection and management.

In 2012, OBiBa and the DataSHaPER project, also from P3G, joined efforts to create the [Maelstrom Research](https://www.maelstrom-research.org) program, whose mission is to develop tools and methods for epidemiological data harmonization. Maelstrom Research is located at the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre, in Montreal, Quebec.

## Contribute

- **Source code**: OBiBa is open source and contributions are welcome. Visit [OBiBa on GitHub](https://github.com/obiba), fork, modify and open pull requests; we will be happy to review them. Before starting major work, contact us to share your ideas so we can guide you.
- **Sponsoring**: [sponsor OBiBa on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/obiba) to help us maintain our test and demo servers.
